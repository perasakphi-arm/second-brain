- **Stakes:** Mid-to-high stakes online cash; specializes in tough, competitive reg-heavy pools
- **Approach:** Practical unexploitability over theoretical GTO; method and habits over spot-specific solutions; self-awareness as the #1 skill to develop

- **Wakko Connection:** Guest on **Mechanics of Poker Podcast Episode 66** — 3-hour deep conversation with Wakko and Adam Carmichael. Topics covered:
  - Why modern players over-complicate poker and hide behind solvers
  - Poker is hard but not complex — solver obsession creates fear and ego
  - Intuition, self-awareness, and simplicity matter more at high stakes than theoretical purity
  - Documenting mistakes is more valuable than celebrating wins
  - Managing downswings, ego, and mental game for long-term success
  - Building a sustainable life around professional poker
- **Core philosophy (directly compatible with Wakko's framework):** Strong, simple GTO foundation → practical unexploitability → exploit where pool can't counter. Mirrors Wakko's Layer 1/2/3 structure.
- **Content:** Weekly GTO Wizard coaching sessions (YouTube), flop exploit series, mindset content. Most recent: "Poker Isn't Complex (You're Just Overthinking It)" (Feb 2026)
