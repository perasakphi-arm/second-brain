# Trends: how NL200 regs create strategy (forums + coaching)

Population read: recurring **workflows** from **Two Plus Two**, **coaching articles**, and **training-site narratives** for online **NL200** (especially Zoom / fast pool). Not a prescription—sources disagree on study vs volume.

**NL500 lens:** Same **stacked** idea (GTO spine → population → bounded exploit → simplify) applies, but coaching and pool narratives stress **smaller edges** and **less rec volume**—see [[concept/nl200-nl500-reg-strategy-big-picture]].

---

## 1. Dual-track model (most common coaching theme)

Public NL200 Zoom material (e.g. Saulo Costa’s write-up on ~1M hands / 5+ bb/100) frames strategy creation as **three stacked layers**, not “one monolithic GTO chart”:

| Layer | Target | How strategy is built (trend) |
|--------|--------|--------------------------------|
| **A. Strong regs** | Avoid spew, avoid unproven meta wars | **GTO literacy + heuristics** (solver-backed shortcuts); daily **drills** to refine patterns (e.g. when small cbet vs check on K72r-type textures). Source: [Getcoach / Saulo Costa article](https://www.getcoach.poker/articles/saulo-costa-i-won-100000-at-200-zoom-here-is-how-to-do-it/) (adapted from video content cited there). |
| **B. Recreationals** | Maximize EV | **Active exploitation**—study population / DB stats (e.g. H2N-style reads), adjust ranges and river calldowns; narrative that recs can be **more aggressive** on some lines than regs. Same article. |
| **C. Weaker regs** | Winrate separation | **Pressure** vs passivity—exploit under-bluffing, under-raising, over-folding to aggression; framed as **mindset + table dynamics**, not only solver trees. Same article. |

**Trend:** Strategy documents at this level are often **opponent-class based** (strong reg / weak reg / rec) before they are “one range for all.”

## 3. Forum: what NL200 grinders actually *do* for “strategy creation”

### Daily structure + accountability

**[From NL200 zoom to never having a 9 to 5 Job](https://forumserver.twoplustwo.com/174/poker-blogs-goals/nl200-zoom-never-having-9-5-job-1795218/)** (chrisgaui, 2021):

- Fixed daily habits: run, pushups, breath work, **study poker** (even 5 minutes if unmotivated).
- **Google Sheets** for stats tracking.
- **HH posting** (Upswing links) for feedback; early self-diagnosis: e.g. **opening too loose when a fish is in the BB**, punting at certain hours → **schedule change**.

**Trend:** Strategy evolves via **public HH review + logging**, not only solo solver.

### Study volume vs play; coaching as inflection

**[19 yo's nl200 challenge](https://forumserver.twoplustwo.com/174/poker-blogs-goals/19-yos-nl200-challenge-1840526/)** (FeitanZoldyck, 2024):

- Stated goals: e.g. **1k hands/day**, **1 hr study/day**, leaderboard / rakeback targets.
- Community pushback: **prioritize study over volume** early in career (disagreement partially accepted).
- Concrete study allocation: **“Most of my studying is in late position configurations”**; awareness of **position elasticity** (same combo different role BTN vs CO 4bp).
- **Coaching** cited as what turned breakeven 50NL into winning 200NL quickly; **table selection** and site-specific Zoom results emphasized.

**Trend:** Serious NL200 projects often **buy a curriculum or coach** to compress the “what to sim first” problem; **LP spots** are a common **first modular focus**.

### Solver workflow (stakes-agnostic but standard for midstakes regs)

**[[GTO] My plan to study using a solver — what could I improve?](https://forumserver.twoplustwo.com/15/poker-theory-amp-gto/my-plan-study-using-solver-what-could-i-improve-1830095/)** (2024):

OP’s intended loop: **~8 hr/week**, **high-frequency spots**, ~**15 representative boards**, write **predicted** frequencies and responses, **compare to solver**, play vs sim in **GTO+**, **cluster similar boards**, short **written summary**, repeat.

Veteran feedback (tombos21): let **data** define “similar flops,” not arbitrary groupings; **test hypotheses** by changing sim inputs; **follow-through** matters more than flop size minutiae; **don’t reinvent**—use **coaching, study groups, courses**.

Verified coach (Brokenstars): **notes + spaced review** matter for retention.

**Trend:** Regs who “use solvers for strategy” converge on **hypothesis → sim → delta → drill → summary**; elite forum advice is **don’t lone-wolf** every node.

## 4. Table count and decision quality (forum meta)

**[Averaging $100/hr at 200nl, 8-10 tabling](https://forumserver.twoplustwo.com/174/poker-blogs-goals/averaging-100-hr-200nl-8-10-tabling-1751015/)**: experienced poster suggests **6-tabling 200NL** instead of 8–10 mixed games for **higher win rate, lower variance, faster improvement**.

**Trend:** Part of “strategy creation” is choosing **enough bandwidth** to apply what you studied; max tabling fights that.

---

## 5. Synthesis: checklist of “what NL200 regs take” when building strategy

Use this as a **pattern list** when comparing your own pipeline:

1. **Segment opponents** (strong reg / weak reg / rec) and keep **playbooks** separate.
2. **Preflop + LP modules first** in study queues (forum + coach path of least regret).
3. **Solver for theory spine** + **written heuristics** + **drills** (GTO Wizard–class tools mentioned in coaching press).
4. **Population / HUD / H2N** (where legal) for **rec and weak-reg exploits**—especially river behavior priors.
5. **HH review** (self + community + coach) with **spot tagging**.
6. **Table count** matched to ability to **execute** exploits you trained.
7. **Buy leverage**: course/coach/study group to avoid re-deriving known sim results (2+2 consensus in solver thread).
