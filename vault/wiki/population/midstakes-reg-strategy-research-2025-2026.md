# What NL200 & NL500 regs are doing with strategy (2025–2026 research)

Synthesizes **dated or explicitly 2025–labeled** public material (training sites, news, articles). It is **not** a scientific sample of all winning regs—it is a **landscape report** for how the **industry narrative** and **observable leaderboard ecology** describe play and study at these stakes.

**Related vault notes:** [[concept/nl200-nl500-reg-strategy-big-picture]] · [[concept/200nl-reg-mda-forum-report]] · [[concept/200nl-reg-strategy-creation-trends]]

---

## Executive summary (2025–2026 lens)

1. **Baseline remains GTO-first for unknowns and strong regs.** Major training content in **2025** still frames “what we’re supposed to do” as the reference, then builds exploits from **deviations** (HUD, DB, population tools)—not the reverse. Example: GTO Wizard’s **“How to Become a Winning Poker Player in 2025”** series opens with **NL500**-raked solver spots to argue that without theory, “exploits” are guesswork, and stresses **non-static** play by villain type ([Part 1](https://blog.gtowizard.com/how-to-become-a-winning-poker-player-in-2025-part-1/), [Part 2](https://blog.gtowizard.com/how-to-become-a-winning-poker-player-in-2025-part-2/)).

2. **NL200 (especially Zoom):** Public coaching still sells a **three-way split**—solid vs crushers, **maximize vs recs**, pressure **weaker regs**—with **drills / heuristics** as the execution layer ([Saulo Costa / NL200 Zoom article](https://www.getcoach.poker/articles/saulo-costa-i-won-100000-at-200-zoom-here-is-how-to-do-it/), dated **Feb 2024** on the page; narrative remains the standard 2025-era Zoom story).

3. **NL500:** **Low bb/100** for even elite winners is a recurring theme; **Zoom 500** is widely described as one of the **hardest** online formats ([PokerTube piece on NL500 Zoom](https://www.pokertube.com/article/is-nl500-zoom-the-toughest-poker-game-in-the-world)). **January 2025** StatName-based reporting still highlights **long-run NL500 crushers** (e.g. “M4D_T1LT” on PokerStars) as reference points for who sits in those games ([PokerListings Jan 2025](https://www.pokerlistings.com/news/who-are-the-top-cash-game-players-in-january-2025)).

4. **Format meta (2025 content):** **Ante cash** adjustments (wider opens, SB limp/fold shifts, more continue vs open) are being taught as a **separate preflop module**—relevant when your 200/500 pool is ante tables ([GTO Wizard — Crush ante cash games](https://blog.gtowizard.com/how_to_crush_ante_cash_games/)).

5. **Study stack (2025 tooling narrative):** **Trainer / drill** mastery, **short focused spots**, **hypothesis before looking at solution**, and **measuring progress**—summarized across GTO Wizard’s 2025 articles and trainer-mastery posts (same blog family; see [How to study GTO solutions](https://blog.gtowizard.com/how-to-study-gto-solutions/), [5 levels of trainer mastery](https://blog.gtowizard.com/the-5-levels-of-trainer-mastery/)).

## NL200 regs — what 2025-era material emphasizes

| Theme | What regs are told to do | Source signal |
|--------|---------------------------|---------------|
| **vs strong regs** | Stay near theory; avoid huge punts and unproven meta wars; build **heuristics** via repetition | Saulo Costa write-up; GTO Wizard 2025 “foundation before exploit” |
| **vs recs** | **Active** exploitation (ranges, rivers); EV still framed as **rec-heavy** in Zoom NL200 | Same Costa article; RIO/PokerStars Zoom primers (older but still cited in 2025 roundups) |
| **Volume vs study** | No single answer; 2025 content pushes **structured study** + **measurable goals** as much as raw hands | GTO Wizard 2025 Parts 1–2; trainer posts |
| **Table load** | Quality of **adaptation** beats autopilot; fewer tables often recommended anecdotally for learning (forum tradition; not year-specific) | See [[concept/200nl-reg-strategy-creation-trends]] |

**Honest gap:** Few **free** sources publish “NL200 reg exact frequencies 2026”; most 2025 advice is **framework + tooling**, not a new public MDA sheet.

## NL500 regs — what 2025-era material emphasizes

| Theme | What regs are told to do | Source signal |
|--------|---------------------------|---------------|
| **Edge size** | Expect **small** winrates vs toughest pools; variance and rake pressure matter | PokerTube on NL500 Zoom; StatName culture |
| **Opponent set** | Mix of **very strong regs** + some weaker players; winrate separation vs **weaker regs** still matters in coaching stories | Costa “destroy weaker regs” framing; GTO Wizard static-vs-adapt KTo 3-bet example at **500NL** rake in 2025 article |

## Cross-stakes: what changed vs “old school 2015” (as told in 2025)

From **GTO Wizard 2025 Part 1** (paraphrased thesis):

- **Exploiting** is still the win-rate driver at all stakes, but **exploits must be grounded** in what balanced play looks like—otherwise adjustments are random.
- **Static** strategies (same buttons vs all types) are explicitly called out as **EV burn**; the prescribed fix is **range vs range + tendencies** questions every spot.
- **NL500** appears in on-site examples (rake model `NL500`, 100bb, Zoom/general trees), i.e. vendors assume **500NL regs consume the same SaaS stack** as everyone else: **pre-solved trees, custom trees, trainers**.

## MDA / population tools in 2025–2026 (where Snap fits)

Public 2025 articles rarely say “**Snap MDA**” by name in search hits; they **do** say:

- Gather **showdown + frequency** evidence, compare to a **benchmark** (solver / population DB), then deviate ([GTO Wizard on building exploits from showdown](https://blog.gtowizard.com/building-exploits-from-showdown/) — linked from 2025 Part 1).

That is **aligned** with **Snap-style MDA**: **leak find → benchmark → bounded rule**. It does **not** support “**only** MDA, no solver” for midstakes regs in 2025 canon.

## Limitations of this research note

- **Marketing bias:** Training sites describe what *students should* do, not verified behavior of all winners.
- **Geography / room:** GG vs PS vs WPN changes pool; 2025 PokerListings piece notes tracking limits at **very high** stakes affecting overall rankings—not NL500 specifically, but reminds that **data source ≠ full market**.
- **Recency:** Some linked NL200 Zoom stories are **2023–2024**; included because they still anchor **2025** coaching SEO and Discord culture.
