# SRP OOP PFC (BB Defend) — Study Task List & Progress Tracker

Task list to fix the [[spot/srp-oop-pfc-bb-defend-mistakes|4 key mistake patterns]] from Tony's BB defend coaching session. Work through modules in order — Module 1 is the highest frequency leak.

---

## Module 1 — Check-Raise for Protection with bdfd

**Goal:** Consistently apply check-raise when the 3-question check confirms: protection needed + bdfd present + bluff ability.

- [ ] **Task 1.1** — Re-study the 5 hands from Mistake 1 (954fd, T73, A85fd, J56fd, J43fd). For each, write one sentence: why check-raise is better than check-call.
- [ ] **Task 1.2** — Memorize the 3-question decision tree: (a) IP over-cbets? (b) Does hand need protection? (c) Do we have bluff ability (bdfd/gutshot)? All yes → check-raise.
- [ ] **Task 1.3** — Drill 10 flop textures (mixed AML/KLL/connected). For each, identify which holdings in your range are check-raise candidates based on the 3 questions.
- [ ] **Task 1.4** — In next 3 sessions, tag every BB defend hand where you chose check-call. After session: was check-raise better?

**Done when:** Zero missed check-raise for protection spots with bdfd over 2 consecutive sessions.

---

## Module 2 — Value Hands Belong in Check-Raise, Not Bluff-Catch

**Goal:** Recognize when top pair or strong mid-pair needs to check-raise for value+protection rather than check-call.

- [ ] **Task 2.1** — Study Hand 3 (853r / A8o). Map out: (a) what is A8o's value? (b) what equity does it deny by check-raising? (c) what does check-calling lose?
- [ ] **Task 2.2** — Build a mental rule: "Am I placing a value hand in bluff-catch because I want to trap — or because check-calling is genuinely better?" If the answer is "trap," confirm protection need first.
- [ ] **Task 2.3** — Drill 5 spots with top-pair or mid-pair hands on connected boards. Decide: check-raise vs check-call. Verify with solver.
- [ ] **Task 2.4** — In next session, mark every check-call with top-pair or better. Post-session review: was any of those a value hand in a protection spot?

**Done when:** Consistently check-raising value hands on protection-needed boards for 2 sessions.

---

## Module 3 — bdfd = Perfect Bluff Candidate for Check-Raise

**Goal:** Automatically identify bdfd combos as check-raise bluff candidates when the spot fits.

- [ ] **Task 3.1** — Study Hands 4 and 10 (993fd/J7bdfd, 972r/Q3bdfd). Understand why bdfd is the "perfect" check-raise bluff: (a) has equity when called, (b) can barrel on flush complete turns, (c) blocks IP's flush draw range.
- [ ] **Task 3.2** — Write the rule: "bdfd + overcard + pool over-cbets = check-raise bluff. Never check-call this."
- [ ] **Task 3.3** — Drill 5 spots: given a bdfd hand on a pool-over-cbet board, decide check-raise vs check-call. Compare with solver.
- [ ] **Task 3.4** — In next session, identify at least 1 bdfd hand that you would previously have check-called and check-raise it instead.

**Done when:** Never check-calling a bdfd perfect-bluff candidate when conditions are met, over 2 sessions.

---

## Module 4 — Hand Selection: Know Your Combo List

**Goal:** Enter every hand with a defined flop plan; check-raise only with the correct combos from Tony's list.

- [ ] **Task 4.1** — Memorize Tony's canonical check-raise bluff combos for Q52-type boards: 53, A4, A3, 65, 75, 43, K3. Understand why each fits (GTO-aligned + concept-matching).
- [ ] **Task 4.2** — For each board type studied (AML, KLL, low-pair, connected), write the top 3–5 check-raise bluff combos. Review against Tony's framework.
- [ ] **Task 4.3** — Preflop drill: before calling BB vs BU, think "what is my flop plan on the 3 most common flop textures?" If you cannot answer → reconsider the call.
- [ ] **Task 4.4** — Review Hand 7 (K9o no bdfd on Q82fd): identify why this was an over-call and what the minimum calling standard is for this spot.

**Done when:** Never check-raising wrong combos; every call has a defined flop plan across 2 sessions.

## Session Review Protocol

After each live session involving BB defend vs BU:

- [ ] Tag every hand where you were BB vs BU in SRP.
- [ ] For each tagged hand, note which mistake category applies (1–4) if any.
- [ ] Add the hand to the relevant study module.
- [ ] At end of week: count mistakes per category. Most frequent → priority for next study session.
