## Preflop

EP opens → BB calls. Pot ~5.5bb.

## Flop: QsQh6h

- Villain c-bets 33%
- Hero **check-raises**
- Hero's hand: pair of 6s (second pair on QQ board) + backdoor flush draw (spades)
- Population read: EP c-bets small at high frequency on paired boards — XR seems justified for protection

## Turn: 4c (brick)

- Villain bets 50% (after calling the check-raise)
- Hero **folds**
- Hero's hand: unchanged — still just pair of 6s, spades bdfd unimproved

## My Thought Process (Self-Assessment)

**Flop:** EP cannot range-bet QQx so a small c-bet is a high-frequency population line. XR looks good for protection. *Error recognized:* [[spot/srp-oop-pfc-bb-defend-mistakes|over-applied aggression]] vs EP — EP's range is tighter than BU, so the CR threshold should be higher.

**Turn:** Assumed EP over-folds vs GTO, so the plan was to play passively (XR-X-Overbet). Folded when villain continued.

**If I replayed:** Would prefer to XR with suited flush draw combos — hearts (e.g., Ah6h, Kh6h) or Ax suited with bdfd. The 8s6s combo has no live flush draw on a heart-heavy board, making it too weak a combo for XR vs EP's tighter range.

## Key Lesson

On paired boards (QQx) vs EP, **check-raise only with flush draw combos (matching board suit)**. Second pair + bdfd in the wrong suit (spades on a heart board) lacks live equity and cannot withstand a turn barrel from EP's stronger range.
