# Pre-Session Leak Focus — 2026-05-02

## Before You Sit Down

1. **3BP OOP PFR — Set/Overpair on Turn**
   Situation: You hold a set or overpair on the turn and IP has Kx/Ax in their range →
   Action: **Check.** Ask "Will IP bet if I check?" If yes → check. Betting only folds out bluffs you want to keep in.

2. **BB Defend vs BU — Check-Raise for Protection (bdfd)**
   Situation: You're BB, IP cbets, your hand has a backdoor flush draw (bdfd) and the board needs protection (flush draw or connected) →
   Action: **Check-raise.** 3-question test: (a) IP over-cbets? (b) Does hand need protection? (c) Do you have bdfd/gutshot? All yes → check-raise. Check-calling concedes initiative.

3. **3BP OOP PFR — Block Bet vs 0-Equity Region**
   Situation: You hold a medium-strength hand (66–TT range) and opponent's range is full of 0-equity hands (no draws, no pair improvement) →
   Action: **Bet 33–50% (block).** Distinguish: if you unblock their bluffs → check-call. If you block their bluffs → block bet.

4. **3BP OOP PFR — Drawy Board + Strong Value = Big Bet**
   Situation: You hold top pair or better on a flop with a flush draw or two connected cards →
   Action: **Bet 87%+ pot and plan to jam turn.** Small bets on drawy boards lose stack-building and let draws realize cheaply.

5. **vs Fish — Run L2 Logic Only**
   Situation: Fish shows multi-street aggression (donk-lead flop + turn + river) →
   Action: **Value-bet when clearly ahead; fold or call when behind. No L3 bluffs.** Fish never folds. GTO indifference logic does not work against a calling station. Sun Tzu: avoid strong, strike weak.
